QRTZ

How it works

The music moves it

The crystal turns with the beat, swells with the bass, sparkles with the treble, and bursts into shards on a drop.

Your pictures, through glass

Open a picture and the crystal refracts it, and its colours become the palette. It runs in your browser: the sound and the picture are analyzed on your computer, and nothing is uploaded.

The full version

The full QRTZ runs in TouchDesigner, with a photo deck, scenes, an operator screen and a phone page.

The demo track, "QRTZ Demo", was written for this page in Ableton Live. Made by scenebiscuit.
