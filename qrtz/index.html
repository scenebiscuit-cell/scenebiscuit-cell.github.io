      Help

      Pick a subject.

      Give it sound

      1. Click Play the demo track, or Open a track to play your own file, or Microphone to listen to the room, or Computer sound to listen to whatever this computer is playing (Spotify, YouTube, DJ software). On the website it needs Chrome or Edge (Firefox and Safari can't share sound with a page), and Chrome asks what to share: for Spotify and other apps (Windows), click the screen under Entire screen and turn on Also share system audio (the apps aren't in the list: their sound comes with the screen); for music in another Chrome tab, pick it under Chrome tab and tick Also share tab audio. A Window brings no sound. Only the sound is used; the picture is never shown or saved.
      2. In the app with DJ software running, turn on Ableton Link there (Rekordbox, Serato, Traktor, Engine DJ, Live): QRTZ follows its tempo and never changes it.
      3. The Inputs card shows the BPM and where it comes from. Within a few seconds the crystal turns with the beat, swells with the bass and sparkles with the treble.

      Give it pictures

      1. Click a picture in the Photos card, or drop pictures anywhere on the window.
      2. The crystal refracts the picture, and the colours come from it.
      3. In the app, the Photos card lists the project's images/ folder; pictures you drop are copied there.

      The screen

      Program (red frame, on the right) is what the audience sees. Preview (green frame, on the left) shows what you've staged, and TAKE between them sends it across. Below: Scene, Crystal and Colours, the media browser (Photos, Album art, Shaders, Textures), Controls and Inputs. The bar at the bottom has the sound and the log. Help and the ? key open this sheet.

      Keys work while this window is in front; click it once first. Greyed ones aren't in the app yet. Ctrl, Alt and Cmd combinations are left to the menus.

      Stage, then TAKE

      1. Set Clicks go to (in the Staged card) to Preview.
      2. Click a scene, crystal, colours, picture, shader, texture, wave shape, or move a slider. It fills amber and Preview shows the result; Program doesn't change.
      3. Stage as many things as you like. Click a staged item again to unstage it; Clear empties the stage.
      4. Press TAKE. It counts down ("LANDS IN 3 BEATS") and everything staged comes into Program together on the next bar.

      Preview or Live

      The Clicks go to switch between the two pictures decides it: Preview (green, pointing left) stages every click, Live (red, pointing right) sends every click straight to Program (switching to it clears the stage). The bar over the panels says which is on, in the same colour. Shift-click sends one click straight to Program. Drop, Blackout and Hanging light sit under Live now: they always act on Program and never on Preview. The keys always act on Program. A staged slider shows a red tick at its live value.

      Bigger Preview

      The arrow button on Preview (Pop out) opens it in its own window, to watch a staged change at a useful size. The full-screen button fills the screen with it; Esc brings the panel back.

      The tempo picks the scene

      Below 100 BPM Organic, from 100 to 135 Grid, above 135 Shards. Every 64 bars the Shaders scene takes a 32-bar turn. A drop cuts to Mandala, Grid or Shards in turn. Scenes change on a bar and crossfade over 4 bars.

      Holding a scene

      1. Click a scene, or press its key (1 to 6). It gets a lock and comes in on the next bar.
      2. While it's held, a dashed outline shows what the tempo would pick, and the card says so.
      3. Click Follow tempo, or press 0, to let the tempo pick again.

      Crystal and colours

      Click a crystal shape (or press N) and the crystal melts into it; it also changes by itself when the music changes section. Pick a palette in Colours; with a picture on, a fixed palette recolours it (P switches back and forth), and From the picture uses its own four colours.

      The photo deck

      1. The Photos strip lists every picture: the demo pictures and, in the app, the images/ folder. LIVE marks the one on screen.
      2. Drop pictures anywhere on the window, or click + Add. In the app they're copied into images/ for good.
      3. A new picture fades in over 4 bars, and the deck moves on by itself every 32 bars. Next (I) moves on now; None (O) turns the photos off.
      4. In the app, Remove moves the picture on screen to the removed/ folder (move it back to undo); Rescan looks in the folder again.

      Album art from Rekordbox (R, app)

      1. Once, in a terminal: python -m pip install pyrekordbox
      2. In Rekordbox, make a playlist called QRTZ with the tracks whose covers you want (without one, QRTZ takes the most recently added tracks).
      3. In QRTZ, pick Album art in the Photos card and click Get covers, or press R.
      4. Up to 40 covers are copied into images/album_art/ and play along with the photos. Rekordbox's own files are only read; running it again follows the playlist.

      Textures inside the crystal (app)

      1. Pick Textures in the Photos card: it lists the crystal_images/ folder.
      2. Click one: the crystal refracts it instead of the picture, while the background stays the picture.
      3. Next (C) moves to the next texture; Off (V) turns them off and on.

      Free assets from Poly Haven (G, app)

      1. On polyhaven.com, open a texture, an HDRI or a model and copy the page's link (for example https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_wall_08).
      2. Click the QRTZ window and press G.
      3. A texture lands in crystal_images/ and shows under Textures; an HDRI's picture lands in images/ and shows under Photos; a model lands in models/ (casting it in crystal is still done by the TouchDesigner project).
      4. The log says what was saved. The assets are CC0: free to use anywhere, gigs included.

      Program on the venue's screens (app)

      1. Plug in the projector, TVs or LED wall processor, and set Windows to Extend these displays (Windows key + P).
      2. In QRTZ's Picture menu, tick Output on Display 2 (or press Ctrl+Shift+2; Ctrl+Shift+1 is the first display, and so on).
      3. That display fills with Program alone; the panel stays on the laptop. Tick more displays for more screens: they all show the same Program.
      4. Untick a display, or Close all outputs, to take outputs away.

      During the show

      Esc on an output turns it into a window; F11 (or double-click) fills the screen again, and Outputs back to full screen does it for all. If a cable comes out, that output closes, the log says so, and Program and the other screens carry on; plug it back in and the output returns. The displays you chose are remembered: next time QRTZ starts, their outputs come back by themselves.

      Good to know

      Outputs render up to 1920 pixels wide (a 4K screen gets that, scaled up). Every screen and the panel share one graphics card, so try your setup at the venue's resolution before the night. With one screen only, Program full screen in this window fills it; on the website, the screen button on Program opens the output as a pop-up window.

      Sliders

      Each multiplies from ×0 to ×2. Warp and Ripple bend the picture; Intensity, Bass, Mids and Treble scale how much the music moves everything. The notch is ×1; double-click a slider, or Reset, to go back.

      Waves

      The picture blends with a mirrored copy of itself in moving waves. Bands, Rings, Spiral or Blobs holds that shape across pictures; Auto gives each new picture another.

      Show buttons

      Drop (J) explodes the crystal and lets it reform. Blackout (B) fades the picture to black over half a second, and back. Hanging light (H) hangs the crystal on a thread from the next bar: it swings with the music and throws light below it. In the Photos card, Shaders holds one shader in the Shaders scene; Take turns lets them rotate again.

      Backup controls from a phone (app)

      1. Put the phone on the same Wi-Fi as the computer.
      2. Open the address shown in the Inputs card (like http://192.168.1.20:9990/) in the phone's browser.
      3. Enter the PIN from config/phone_pin.txt on this computer. Five wrong PINs lock that phone out for a minute.
      4. Tap a scene or Follow tempo, Next photo, Hanging light, Blackout or Drop: they go live on Program at once.
      5. Add photos sends pictures to the deck; the newest is staged in Preview, for a TAKE.

      Good to know

      The TouchDesigner project serves the same page on the same port, so only one of the two can run it at a time; the other says "Phone page: off" and carries on. The first time, Windows may ask whether to let QRTZ use the network: allow Private networks.

      Inputs card

      The BPM and where it comes from (Ableton Link in the app, or the beat in the audio), the beat light, the bass, mids and treble meters with the auto gain, and the live crystal. The Level meter in the title bar is the whole mix before the auto gain.

      Flags

      Green All inputs working; amber Link off · audio beat (no Link heard, the beat comes from the audio) or No beat yet; red No sound after 3 seconds of silence. The most urgent also shows as a chip in the title bar.

      Log

      The bar at the bottom shows the last three messages: what each click and key did, outputs opening, a display unplugged. All shows the whole session, newest first.
